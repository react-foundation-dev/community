# React Community Travel Fund

The React Community Travel Fund supports travel and attendance at events and activities directly related to React technologies, or goals. The fund supports travel for collaborators who are at least 18 years old and recognized as adults in both the country of departure and the destination country, based on local laws. Applicants should explore employer-sponsored funding options as their first source of financial support. 


## What is Covered

* **Airfare/Train/Ferry:** In economy class only.
* **Transportation:** Ground transportation to/from the airport to the hotel/event venue.
* **Lodging:** Dates of the event plus one night prior are eligible for reimbursement.
* **Visa fees** (even in case the visa is denied).

NOTE: Funds may not be used for miscellaneous travel expenses including food, non-airport transportation, baggage fees, etc.


## Application and Reimbursement Process

1. **Cost Estimation:** Calculate anticipated expenses in accordance with these guidelines.
2. **Pre-Approval Request:** Submit the cost estimate and a brief justification for travel (e.g., speaking engagements or event support) by completing the application **[here](https://forms.gle/UYNv2KiYvyaXvqA27)**. Please see [Deadlines and Timelines](#deadlines-and-timelines) for information about cut-off dates. 
3. **Approval & Authorization:** Once your request has been reviewed, you will be notified of the outcome. 
4. **Booking:** Proceed with booking travel arrangements following authorization from the Executive Director or Project staff.
5. **Expense Reimbursement:** After the event, kindly submit your expenses within 30 days. Submit the [Linux Foundation expense report](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/d/1s7dAyiiEitXw_uPeiUQkBYC7tsFvQ0vvQToVMKsGIu4/edit), [wire transfer form](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nNYtpoW6GvI7IwGUhDrf0znYH4eT9ffIyGZPTTjx5vk/copy), and all supporting receipts to [staff@react.foundation](mailto:staff@react.foundation). Please note that the Foundation operates on a reimbursement-only basis. Contact [staff@react.foundation](mailto:staff@react.foundation) for templates or assistance.

## Deadlines and Timelines

The default deadline for submitting a community fund request is 8 weeks prior to the travel date to allow for all requests for an event to be reviewed at the same time. If you've missed a deadline, or if a deadline was impossible to meet, please submit your request anyway, explaining why you weren't able to meet the deadline if possible.
